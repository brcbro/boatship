Short version: this website does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It only relies on a small amount of essential browser storage to work properly.

1. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. "Session storage" is similar, but is cleared automatically when you close the browser tab. Websites use them to remember things between pages.

2. What this website uses

NameTypePurposeDuration
cohortix-loader-seen Session storage Skips the intro animation after you have seen it once during your visit Until you close the tab
ct-booking Session storage Holds your booking summary so the confirmation page can show it, without putting your details in the page address Until you close the tab
Cloudflare security cookies (e.g. __cf_bm) Strictly necessary cookie May be set by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, to tell real visitors apart from bots and protect the site Up to 30 minutes

None of these are used to track you across websites, build a profile of you, or show you ads.

3. Third-party websites

Our pages link to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub, X and the websites of clients in our portfolio. These sites do not set cookies on our website, but once you visit them, their own cookie and privacy policies apply.

4. Managing storage

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Because we only use essential storage, blocking it will not stop you browsing the site. The booking confirmation page may simply show less detail.

5. Changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
